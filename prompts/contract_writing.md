## This is a contract, matched to one of Alan's contract offers

A contractor is bought as a specialist for a defined piece of work. Lead with
the evidence behind this offer, in the order the ad describes its work, and
keep the rest brief. Do not argue for a long-term fit or a career path; argue
that he can deliver this work in three to six months. A run of short
contracts is evidence for that, not against it. Never invent a title or a
certification the evidence does not hold.
