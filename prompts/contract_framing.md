## This is a contract: score it the way a contractor is bought

The ad is for a contract or fixed-term role. A contractor is bought as a
specialist for a defined piece of work, not hired for a career, so judge it
differently from a permanent role:

1. **Match the ad to one of `assets.contract_offers`**, by what the work
   actually is, and return that offer's id as `contract_offer`. If none fits,
   return null and say so in the rationale: that is usually a skip.
2. **Judge whether he could deliver that work in three to six months**, from
   the offer's evidence. That, not the permanent-role target list, is what
   `target_role_match` means here: true when an offer fits.
3. **A pattern of short contracts is not a weakness here.** For a contract it
   is the evidence that he lands, delivers and leaves cleanly. Do not raise
   tenure as a challenge point.
4. **Pay is judged as a day rate** in the hard filters above. Do not convert
   it to an annual salary, and do not compare it with the salary floor.
5. **`emphasise` leads with the matched offer's evidence**, in the order the
   ad's own work is described.

Never invent an offer, and never claim a title or certification the evidence
does not hold.
